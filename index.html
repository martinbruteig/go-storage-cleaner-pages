Questions

Does GO Storage Cleaner access my Pokémon GO account?

No. There is no login and no connection to the game. The app only generates search strings that you copy and paste into Pokémon GO yourself.

Does it transfer Pokémon for me?

No. The final search shows transfer candidates for you to review. Every transfer is your own decision, made manually in the game.

How much does it cost?

The app is free to download. Generating search strings requires Pro: $2.99 per month, $14.99 per year, or $29.99 as a one-time purchase (US prices; local prices are shown in the App Store).

Do I have to create tags in Pokémon GO?

No. Tags give the most thorough protection, but Quick Clean works without them: you favorite what you want to keep, and favorites are always left out of the review list.

Which language does it use?

English. The app is in English, and the search strings use English search keywords, so set the game language to English when you paste them.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone. GO Storage Cleaner is made for iPhone and is available in the App Store.
